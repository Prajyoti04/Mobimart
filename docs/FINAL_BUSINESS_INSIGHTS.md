# Final Business Insights

### Store differentiation
Stores have materially different demand profiles and should not receive uniform stock.

### Forecast and allocation
Forecast-driven allocation prioritizes demand and store/product characteristics rather than proportional distribution.

### Lifecycle
EOL candidates are identified from declining demand and successor relationships. They are recommendations, not contractual discontinuation notices.

### Markdown
Markdown levels are transparent prototype policy assumptions intended to accelerate clearance of declining products.

### Transfer
Store-level transfer recommendations prioritize stores where successor demand already exists or predecessor demand creates a transition opportunity.

### Simulation
The optimizer was stress-tested for low, normal, high and festive demand across 10 stochastic runs over six weeks.

### Main finding
The optimized policy performs better than the naive baseline, but the simulation exposes insufficient starting inventory as the main constraint. The next production decision is therefore to balance service-level improvement against working-capital capacity.
